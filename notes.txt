Reaction card balance:
Reaction cards should usually cost 0–2 Chi.
They can be stronger than normal cards only when their condition is narrow.
Flow cards should be slightly weaker than pure Reaction cards because they can be played in both Action Phase and Reaction Phase.

A card with Flow can be played during both Action Phase and Reaction Phase.
Flow cards should usually be flexible, defensive or adaptive effects.
Flow should mostly belong to Water.
A flow card should have at least one of these reasons to play it in the Reaction Phase:
- uses leftover Chi before refreshing,
- saves/finishes a unit before the next turn,
- has a bonus if played in the Reaction Phase,
- targets units with summonedThisTurn,
- protects against future turn start effects.

Chi rule:
Each player has one Chi pool.
Chi refreshes at the start of that player's Action Phase.
Unspent Chi can be used during that player's next Reaction Phase.
Reaction cards spend the same Chi as normal cards.

Summoning rule:
Units summoned during Action Phase or Reaction Phase cannot attack until the player's next Action Phase, unless the card has a special keyword

Current Reaction Phase rule:
Reactions do not interrupt attacks or spells.
Damage resolves immediately.
Dead units are removed before Reaction Phase.
Reaction cards can only affect units that survived the Action Phase.

Current visual rules:

- Reaction = light blue background
- Fire = red border
- Water = dark blue border
- Earth = earthy green border
- Air = cream/yellow border

Implemented:
- Swift: unit can attack the turn it is played.
- Buff: move can give stats to a friendly unit.
- Guard: enemy attacks must target Guard units first.
- Dodge: prevents the first damage instance dealt to this unit.
- Ignite: bonus activates if you damaged the enemy hero earlier this turn. Ignite does not activate from the same card's damage.
- damage_enemy_hero moves auto-cast without target selection.

Latest update:

- Implemented Wind Slice: selects two enemy units and deals damage to both.
- Sweeping Strikes works as an AoE move and supports Momentum.
