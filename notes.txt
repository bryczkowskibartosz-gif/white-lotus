Core rules:

Chi rule:
Each player has one Chi pool.
Chi refreshes at the start of that player's Action Phase.
Unspent Chi can be used during that player's next Reaction Phase.
Reaction cards spend the same Chi as normal cards.

Summoning rule:
Units summoned during Action Phase or Reaction Phase cannot attack until the player's next Action Phase, unless the card has Swift.

Reaction Phase rule:
Reactions do not interrupt attacks or moves.
Damage resolves immediately.
Dead units are removed before Reaction Phase.
Current enemy-targeted reactions can only affect enemy units that were summoned this turn.

Hand and deck rules:
Max hand size is 10.
If a player draws with a full hand, the drawn card is burned.
If a player draws from an empty deck, that player takes fatigue damage.
Fatigue damage starts at 1 and increases by 1 each time that player draws from an empty deck.

Implemented mechanics:
- Battlecry: effect triggers after playing a unit.
- Supported Battlecry effects:
  - Draw a card type.
  - Draw a card type with a specific keyword.
  - Buff self.
  - Gain hero health.
  - Buff a random other friendly unit.
  - Damage a random enemy unit.
- Swift: unit can attack the turn it is played.
- Buff: move can give stats to a friendly unit.
- Guard: enemy attacks must target Guard units first.
- Dodge: prevents the first damage instance dealt to this unit.
- Ignite: bonus activates if you damaged the enemy hero earlier this turn.
- Momentum: bonus activates if you played a reaction card during your last Reaction Phase.
- Flow: move can be played during both Action Phase and Reaction Phase.
- Fortify: one-time Earth bonus that activates at the start of your Action Phase if the unit survived since being played.
- Ice Blast: damages an enemy unit and prevents its next attack.
- Sweeping Strikes: deals damage to all enemy units. Momentum repeats the damage as a second damage instance.
- Wind Slice / Pebble Toss / Splitting Rock: choose two enemy units and damage both.
- damage_enemy_hero moves auto-cast without target selection.
- gain_hero_health moves auto-cast without target selection.

Element identity:

Fire:
- Aggressive.
- Strong Action Phase.
- Direct damage.
- High attack, lower health.
- Wants to damage the enemy hero.
- Uses Ignite.
- Watch if Fire has enough refill without becoming too explosive.

Air:
- Trickster / tempo element.
- Uses reactions.
- Uses Momentum.
- Dodges, exhausts, weakens, destroys or disrupts newly summoned units.
- Should create cool combo turns, but can have dead cards if sequencing is bad.

Earth:
- Defensive.
- High health.
- Guard units.
- Fortify and buffs.
- Stabilizes the board.
- Watch if Guard + buffs creates board locks too easily.

Water:
- Flexible and adaptive.
- Uses Flow.
- Healing, small damage, protection and recovery.
- Should be useful in both Action Phase and Reaction Phase.
- Watch if Water has enough proactive pressure and not only passive stabilization.

Reaction card balance notes:
Reaction cards should usually cost 0–2 Chi.
They can be stronger than normal cards only when their condition is narrow.
Flow cards should be slightly weaker than pure Reaction cards because they can be played in both Action Phase and Reaction Phase.
3+ Chi reactions should be rare and should feel like big swing cards.
